Lewis & Short Lexicon: self-hosted copy

Upload this whole "lewis-short" folder to your web host, keeping the "data" folder next to index.html.
Then link to it (https://YOUR-SITE/lewis-short/) or embed it with:

  <iframe src="https://YOUR-SITE/lewis-short/" title="Lewis & Short Latin Dictionary"
          style="width:100%;height:85vh;border:0;border-radius:16px" loading="lazy"></iframe>

It must be served from a web server (it loads its data files); opening index.html
directly from your computer will not work.

Text: Lewis & Short, A Latin Dictionary (1879), digitized by the Perseus Project, Tufts University (CC BY-SA).
